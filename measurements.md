## What to capture per bed

- **Length** along the contour (the long dimension, across the fall line)
- **Width** of the killed strip — spot-check it at a few points, don't assume uniform
- **Pieces/breaks** — the two inner beds are tarped in sections around the spared bee-balm
  clumps; get each piece's length separately
- **Area** (or just give me length × width per piece and I'll total it)
