Plan view from above — north is up, the road edge is at the left and the slope rises to the toe (top of the slope) at the right. The fall line runs left–right and is stopped at each 12″ grass contour band (the green columns). Beds are drawn to the measured as-built scale — each tarped patch to its measured length and width — and the two inner south beds are broken around the spared bee-balm clumps (the dashed green gaps). Colored by flower color; hover or tap for a patch or clump. Source: gardenmap.md.

  • Green columns = 12″ grass contour bands (road, mid, toe) — a staple-anchored erosion scaffold that runs the full slope length, left standing until the meadow's roots take over, then removed.
  • Dashed green gaps inside the two inner south beds = the established bee-balm / bergamot clumps, spared and cut around (not re-sown).
  • Patch lengths and widths are as measured (2026-07-23). The gap lengths between patches are inferred to fill each bed's ~41 ft run — the tarped pieces themselves are to scale. Hatched panel = juglone zone (walnut corner); dashed panel = butterfly territory.
