# Cedar-Slope Feeders

Species that **forage on the cedar (west) slopes — nectar, pollen, or seed — but complete
their life cycle elsewhere.** The forage side of the habitat; companion to `breeders.md` (the
species that reproduce here). Bloom *timing* lives in the
[bloom calendar](https://dev-grimnir.github.io/meadow-public/calendar.html) — this doc is the
**visitor roster**, not a second calendar.

**Scope:** the cedar slopes only.

---

## The feeders

- **Fall-migrant Monarchs** (*Danaus plexippus*) — the asters and blazing star are the
  refueling stop in the late-Sept → Oct migration week. (The summer breeding generation is in
  `breeders.md`; this is the through-traffic.)
- **Ruby-throated Hummingbird** (*Archilochus colubris*) — works the red, tubular flowers:
  Scarlet Bee Balm and Wild Columbine. Migratory; nests off-site.
- **Eastern Tiger Swallowtail** (*Papilio glaucus*) — nectars across the bloom; breeds on the
  property's trees (wild cherry, tulip poplar, ash), not our plantings.
- **Painted Lady** (*Vanessa cardui*) and the other *Vanessa* ladies — transient nectarers; no
  host in the palette, and they don't overwinter here.
- **Honeybees** (*Apis mellifera*) — managed colonies; forage the whole bloom, especially the
  mountain mint and the asters.
- **The generalist crowd** — the native bees, wasps, hoverflies, soldier beetles and various
  butterflies a flat-topped nectar bar pulls in. **Mountain Mint** draws the widest range of
  any plant here; the asters and yarrow are the other big generalist plates.

## Seed & winter food

The standing seed heads feed birds through the winter — the other half of the leave-the-stems
rule (the first half being the overwintering insects in `breeders.md`).

- **American Goldfinch** (*Spinus tristis*) and other seed-eaters work the **Black-Eyed Susan,
  asters, Blazing Star, milkweed, and yarrow** seed heads from fall into winter. Don't cut them
  down — that's the food.

## Forage highlights

- **Mountain Mint** — the broadest draw on the slope; the generalist magnet.
- **Asters (New England + Smooth Blue)** — the fall monarch fuel window; time-critical.
- **Scarlet Bee Balm + Wild Columbine** — the red-tubular pair for hummingbirds, through
  summer and the migration edges.

For *when* each blooms, see the
[bloom calendar](https://dev-grimnir.github.io/meadow-public/calendar.html).
